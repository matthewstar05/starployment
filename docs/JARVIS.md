# JARVIS

Settings → JARVIS has four modes. Save settings to activate a mode.

- **OFF** uses your ordinary automatic-application preferences.
- **NEW** considers suitable postings published in the last 24 hours, newest first, and sends using saved answers. If a posting has no publication date, its discovery date is used. Required unknown answers stop the application.
- **ALL** balances newest postings and highest match scores across new jobs and unfinished prepared drafts, of any age, as long as a scan found the posting still listed in the last three days (old but still hiring). It prepares applications, runs AI Autofill, authorizes the resulting answers, and sends without a separate review. Turning on ALL is standing permission to send those AI answers, including labeled assumptions. They stay with that application and never enter your saved answer bank.
- **ALL+** (`all_plus`) is ALL for nights away from the Mac: a wider net and full speed. It also takes postings whose only review reason is "Posting date is over 180 days old" (they must still be listed, as in ALL), and allows twice `company_cap.count` per employer. It uses your saved concurrency unhalved and halves the scan interval (minimum five minutes). It defers only when the one-minute load passes 1.1 per performance core or free memory drops under 10% (`jarvis.PLUS_*`), for at most three minutes in a row, so it slows down before the Mac freezes but rarely stops.

Both modes, like ordinary automatic applications, skip an employer once it has `company_cap.count` attempts in `company_cap.days` (default 3 in 30) and alternate role families (software, data, quant, finance, accounting, business, risk, economics) so one kind of role or one large employer cannot take every overnight send. Change the mode from the JARVIS badge in the sidebar or in Settings.

Both modes respect the minimum match score, eligibility checks, priority companies, and explicit stars. JARVIS excludes Workday and unsupported forms. It never retries a job with an existing application attempt, never solves security challenges, and records success only after employer confirmation. Incomplete applications remain available for review. Practice mode still prevents submission.

NEW and ALL halve saved send, preparation, and per-application agent concurrency (rounded down, minimum one), halve the global agent-process ceiling, and reduce source and board concurrency. They keep the saved scan interval: a scan of every board takes several minutes, so the old halved interval kept the Mac scanning almost nonstop. Their scan-and-apply worker runs at macOS background quality of service (efficiency cores, throttled disk; `governor.set_thread_qos`, carried into pool threads with `governor.same_qos`), so it yields to whatever you are doing. ALL+ runs it at utility. Your saved values are preserved for OFF. Scans and new sends defer when the resource governor reports high load or low available memory, for at most ten minutes in a row (`jarvis.MAX_DEFER_SECONDS`); after that they go ahead at the reduced concurrency, so a Mac that stays loaded slows JARVIS down but never stops it. Running browser actions finish normally; resource limits reduce contention but cannot guarantee zero impact from the browser or other apps.

**Set aside, not retried.** A posting JARVIS has to leave for you (a required transcript or video upload, an answer it may not give, saved answers missing in NEW, a posting it could not verify) is recorded in `jarvis_holds` and skipped by later cycles, with one activity-log line instead of one per cycle. It comes back when its draft changes (you answered it, or saved answers changed) or when its time runs out: three days for a reason only you can fix, one day for an unverifiable posting, two hours for something that may pass on its own, such as an AI call that failed while the Mac was busy (`jarvis.SET_ASIDE_SECONDS`). Before this, once fresh postings ran out JARVIS spent every cycle retrying the same blocked drafts and sent nothing until new postings arrived.

JARVIS uses background browsers and keeps the Mac from idle sleeping while Starployment is open. The display may sleep. Closing the app, shutting down, or closing the laptop lid can stop overnight work. The existing applications-per-run cap and one-employer-per-cycle limit remain in force. The activity log records JARVIS authorizations and confirmed outcomes.
